**Format as implemented** (`backend/app/celpip_format.py`):

- Listening (≈46–55 min, 6 parts, 38 scored items): Problem Solving 8 · Daily Life Conversation 5 · Listening for
  Information 6 · News Item 5 · Discussion 8 · Viewpoints 6.
- Reading (≈43–56 min, 4 parts, 38 items): Correspondence 11 · Apply a Diagram 8 · Reading for Information 9 ·
  Reading for Viewpoints 10.
- Writing (53 min): Writing an Email 27 min · Responding to Survey Questions 26 min (150–200 words each).
- Speaking (≈15–20 min, 8 tasks, prep/response seconds): Giving Advice 30/90 · Personal Experience 30/60 ·
  Describing a Scene 30/60 · Making Predictions 30/60 · Comparing and Persuading 60 selection + 60/60 · Difficult
  Situation 60/60 · Expressing Opinions 30/90 · Unusual Situation 30/60.
- Scoring: a separate level 0–12 per skill; levels 3–12 correspond to the same CLB level.

The app does **not** reproduce official scoring rules. Its rubric descriptors are our own and are labelled unofficial.
